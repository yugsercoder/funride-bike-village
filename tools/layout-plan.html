FunRide PH · Rizal Park, Manila

Site
Overview

Whole event site from KM 0 on Roxas Boulevard to the finish on Independence Road. The red dotted lines are the rider / guest flow lanes.

Booth type

  • Tent with back siding
  • Open tent
  • Merchant (open tent)
  • Booth stall only
  • Big booth
  • Aircon tent (VIP)
  • Stage

Key points

  1. Start: KM 0, Roxas Boulevard. Satellite Stage (12 × 12 ft, 3 ft high) on the KM 0 plaza.
  2. Standby per Wave feeds Exit to Start and the ramp up to KM 0.
  3. Entrance southeast corner, Exit southwest corner.
  4. Finish on Independence Road, beside BYD Garage and BYD Test Drive.
  5. BYD Test Drive stays on Independence Road and stops at the South Drive corner.
  6. DOT, Oxecure / Dr. O, BDO and Specialign are branded tents with an 8 × 4 ft photo wall and 3D letters on their east side.
  7. BYD Showroom: 10 × 15 m (32.8 × 49.2 ft) truss tent with arch; AC Health / AC Mobility booth removed.
  8. BYD: Garage + Test Drive tents on the north half of Independence Road; the south half is the test drive lane out to South Drive.
  9. Artists come in through the Artists Entrance and follow the path to the aircon tents.
